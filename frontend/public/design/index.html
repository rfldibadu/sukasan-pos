How to Review and Approve Prototypes:
  1. Open any prototype: Each page is fully self-contained. You can click "Phone Frame" to preview immediately inside the simulated device, or click "Open Screen" for full screen.
  2. Interactive Controls: Try clicking category tabs, opening the bottom sheets (drawers), or opening the Add/Edit modals.
  3. Provide Feedback: If any adjustments to buttons, layout, fields, or colors are desired, mention them. Once satisfied, we proceed to implement the page in Next.js React code!
